Darkest Vow

A dark fantasy tap/idle RPG. Choose a Champion, recruit a warband, defeat bosses and earn lasting power.

Get help

Email farid.jafarlee@gmail.com. Include your app version, device model and the steps that led to the problem. Screenshots can help. Never send passwords.

Progress and saves

Progress is saved locally on the device where you play. Keep the app installed to retain it; cloud sync is not available. If saving fails, the game preserves its last saved state and offers Save / retry storage in Settings.

Offline Gold

Your warband earns Gold between visits, subject to the game's offline-income cap. Returning to the app does not automatically clear stages.
