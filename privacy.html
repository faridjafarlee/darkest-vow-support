Darkest Vow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Darkest Vow is a single-player tap/idle RPG developed by Farid Jafarli.

Game data stays on your device

Your chosen Champion name, game progress and settings are stored locally. The game does not send those details to the developer. It contains no third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, accounts or cloud-sync service. It does not request access to your camera, microphone or photo library.

Storage and removal

Progress remains on the device where you play. Deleting the app removes its local data. Apple may include app data in device backups according to your settings. Clearing browser storage removes progress in the browser preview.

Apple services

Apple handles App Store distribution, TestFlight feedback and diagnostics under Apple's own policies and your settings. Any information you choose to submit through TestFlight is processed by Apple and may be shared with the developer to investigate your report. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Support messages

If you contact support, the email address and information you provide are used to respond and investigate the issue. Please do not send passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information.

This website

This support site has no advertising or analytics scripts. The hosting provider processes standard web requests under its own privacy policy.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated if the game's data practices change. Questions or requests: farid.jafarlee@gmail.com.
